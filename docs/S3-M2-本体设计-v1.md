# S3 M2 本体语义层设计（v1 草案 · 脊柱先行）

> 依据：S3 方案 v1.0 + 安平四资产 + 现有本体模式（Pydantic + own() + LinkTypeDef + 动作模板）。
> 原则：本体优先（业务对象视角），表为字段语义参考；脊柱先行（先主线 8-12 对象跑通再补全到 30-35）。
> 执行真相源在本体（M2），DES 初稿仅参考（ADR 0007）。

## 一、演示主线（脊柱）核心对象（首版 12 个）

### 客户域
1. **Customer**（单一客户）——PK=customer_id；源 ap_customer
   - 字段：customer_id, name, org_id, org_name, cert_type, cert_no(脱敏), internal_rating, external_rating, industry, asset_quality, established_at, reg_capital, chairman, legal_rep, address
   - 链接：belongs_to_group(N:1 GroupCustomer), has_warning_signals(1:N WarnSignal), has_collateral(1:N Collateral), has_concentration(1:N ConcentrationLimit)
2. **GroupCustomer**（集团客户）——PK=group_customer_id；源 ap_group_customer
   - 字段：group_customer_id, name, group_type, member_count, total_exposure, risk_level
   - 链接：contains(1:N Customer), has_warning_signals(1:N WarnSignal)

### 风险监测域
3. **WarningSignal**（预警信号）——PK=warning_id；源 ap_warning_signal
   - 字段：warning_id, customer_id, group_customer_id, org_id, warn_level(RED/YELLOW/BLUE), event_type, warn_source, warn_reason, signal_status(GENERATED/CONFIRMED/GRADED/IN_DISPOSAL/CLOSED), topic_1, topic_2, signal_desc, generated_at
   - 链接：for_customer(N:1 Customer), for_group(N:1 GroupCustomer), has_disposal(1:N Disposal)
4. **Metric**（维度指标）——PK=metric_id；源 ap_dim_metric
   - 字段：metric_id, dim_type, cust_no, group_cust_no, biz_type, indx_id, indx_name, indx_value, indx_unit, currency, data_date
   - 链接：for_customer(N:1 Customer)

### 预警处置域
5. **Disposal**（处置）——PK=disposal_id；源 ap_disposal
   - 字段：disposal_id, warning_id, disposal_type, disposal_status(DRAFT/SUBMITTED/APPROVING/APPROVED/REJECTED/EXECUTING/DONE), comment, deal_user, create_time
   - 链接：for_warning(N:1 WarningSignal), has_approval(1:N ApproveOrder)
6. **Collateral**（押品）——PK=collateral_id；源 ap_collateral
   - 字段：collateral_id, customer_id, collateral_type, estimated_value, appraisal_date, valuation_status, bank_detail, securities_detail
   - 链接：for_customer(N:1 Customer)

### 审批域
7. **ApproveOrder**（审批单）——PK=order_id；源 ap_approve_order
   - 字段：order_id, order_type, order_title, apply_user_id, approved_user_id, order_status(PROCESS/APPROVED/REJECTED), business_type, remark, opinion, apply_time, approve_time
   - 链接：for_disposal(N:1 Disposal), has_tasks(1:N ApproveTask)
8. **ApproveTask**（审批任务）——PK=task_id；源 ap_approve_task
   - 字段：task_id, order_id, node_id, node_name, node_seq, post_id, approve_rule, task_status, assignee
   - 链接：of_order(N:1 ApproveOrder)

### 集中度域
9. **ConcentrationLimit**（集中度限额）——PK=limit_id；源 ap_concentration_limit
   - 字段：limit_id, cust_no, customer_name, concentration_limit, limit_status, approve_status, approve_comment
   - 链接：for_customer(N:1 Customer)

### 共债域
10. **CoDebtCustomer**（共债客户）——PK=codebt_id；源 ap_codebt_customer
    - 字段：codebt_id, customer_id, codebt_count, total_debt, risk_score
    - 链接：for_customer(N:1 Customer)

### 风险项目域
11. **RiskProject**（风险项目）——PK=project_id；源 ap_risk_project
    - 字段：project_id, group_customer_name, division, project_name, business_type, credit_subject, business_balance, risk_exposure, impairment_provision, five_classification, guarantee_method, project_progress
    - 链接：for_group(N:1 GroupCustomer), has_approval(1:N ApproveOrder)

### 机构/用户域
12. **Organization**（机构）——PK=org_id；源 ap_org
    - 字段：org_id, org_name, org_type(集团/银行/证券/保险/信托/租赁/基金), parent_id, risk_role
    - 链接：has_users(1:N User), is_risk_monitor_of(1:N Customer)
